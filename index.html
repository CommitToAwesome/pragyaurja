Audit Report Results
CRITICAL DEFECT #1 Pages 32 & 33

Duplicate Essay Printed Under Two Names

The exact essay titled "From Home Fixes to Mega Projects" was printed twice on consecutive pages, credited to Shubh Agrawal (p.32) and Dhanraj Jain (p.33).

✓ REDESIGN STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: Mandatory digital tracking via Adobe InDesign Links panel & digital pre-flight checks prior to PDF export. Automated script cross-checks text hashes.
LAYOUT DEFECT #2 Pages 5 to 9

5 Consecutive Pages of Executive Desk Messages

Five full pages filled with tiny passport photos and centered, dense administrative text create a massive barrier to reader engagement.

✓ REDESIGN STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: Consolidate administrative speeches into a single 2-page spread titled "Leadership Perspectives" using synthesized Q&A highlights and environmental portraiture.
VISUAL DEFECT #3 Pages 65 to 73

Uncurated Smartphone Lab Dumps

Section "Blueprints Beyond" contained dark, unedited photos of empty labs paired with generic stock quotes ("Silence of library...").

✓ REDESIGN STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: Purge static empty room photos. Replace with active documentary photojournalism featuring students in motion, shot with prime lenses and color-graded in Lightroom.
TYPOGRAPHY DEFECT #4 Publication-Wide

Centered Long-Form Copy & Font Mixing

Centered alignment applied to multi-line poetry and body copy forced reader eyes to search for line starts. Random system fonts and drop shadows ruined cohesion.

✓ REDESIGN STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: Enforce strict left-aligned, rag-right body text set at 10pt/13.5pt leading. Establish 3 font families max with strict hierarchy guidelines.
